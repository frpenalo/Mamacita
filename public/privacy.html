Privacy Policy

Softmedia, LLC (operating as Mamacita) · Last updated October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Softmedia, LLC, operating the Mamacita platform ("Mamacita", "we", "us"), collects and uses information when we send appointment and waitlist notifications on behalf of participating barbershops. By providing your phone number to a participating barbershop, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Your name and mobile phone number, which you provide when you call or visit a barbershop to join the waitlist or book an appointment.
  • Basic details about your visit (the shop, your place in line, and appointment time) needed to send you relevant updates.

How we use your information

We use your information only to send you transactional updates about your own visit: confirmation that you were added to the waitlist, appointment confirmations and reminders, and a notice when your turn is approaching. We do not use it for marketing or promotional messaging.

SMS / text messaging program

  • Consent (opt-in): You opt in verbally when you call or visit the barbershop and provide your own mobile number to be added to the waitlist or to book an appointment. Messages are sent only to customers who provided their own number.
  • Types of messages: waitlist confirmation, appointment confirmation, appointment reminders, and turn-approaching notices.
  • Message frequency varies based on your activity with the shop.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number or opt-in / consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used solely to deliver the notifications described above. Information may be shared only with service providers (such as our messaging provider) strictly to deliver these messages, and when required by law.

Data retention

We retain your information only as long as needed to provide these notifications and to meet legal and recordkeeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To request access to or deletion of your information, contact us using the details below.
